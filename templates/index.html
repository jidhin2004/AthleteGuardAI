Project Scope

Why AthleteGuard AI?

Athletes often monitor training performance but may overlook important health factors such as sleep, fatigue, stress and previous injuries. AthleteGuard AI brings these factors together and uses machine learning to estimate injury risk based on the submitted health information.

MONITOR

Track daily health and training parameters including sleep hours, resting heart rate, fatigue, and stress levels.

PREDICT

Use Random Forest machine learning classification to estimate injury risk accurately based on submitted health metrics.

UNDERSTAND

View an easy-to-understand prediction result with feature breakdown, historical trends, and weekly summaries.

Explainable Outputs

Risk Classification Categories

The risk category represents an estimated injury-risk level based on the submitted health information.

LOW RISK

Low probability of muscle fatigue or joint strain. Biometric indicators and recovery metrics are in optimal ranges.

MEDIUM RISK

Moderate workload strain detected. High fatigue or elevated heart rate observed. Workload reduction and active recovery advised.

HIGH RISK

High neuromuscular strain and elevated risk of strain. Immediate rest, hydration, and medical check advised before intense sessions.
